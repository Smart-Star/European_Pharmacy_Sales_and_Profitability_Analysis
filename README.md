## 📌 Project Overview
This project provides commercial stakeholders with actionable insights into pharmacy performance across multiple European countries. The dashboard evaluates revenue patterns, identifies low/high margin product categories, assesses promotional effectiveness, and tracks localized branch metrics.

---

## 🎯 Key Business Questions Addressed
- **Temporal & Seasonal Trends:** How do revenue, units sold, and profit margins change over time?
- **Geographic Breakdown:** Which countries and regions drive the highest total revenue and profit margins?
- **Location Analytics:** How do sales volume and profitability differ between Urban, Suburban, and Rural pharmacies?
- **Product & Brand Performance:** Which product categories yield the best profit margins versus volume?
- **Promotional Impact:** How do promotional campaigns impact baseline sales volume vs. profit margin?

---

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies Used
- **Power BI Desktop:** Data modeling, DAX measure creation, interactive visual design, and row-level drill-down.
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions):** Custom measures for profit margin %, time-intelligence calculations, and performance metrics.
- **Power Query:** Data transformation, dynamic data type formatting, and structural cleanup.

## 💡 Key Insights & Recommendations
- **Geographic Focus:** Top performing regions contribute to over X% of gross margin.
- **Product Optimization:** Identified low-margin, high-volume product categories for potential pricing or vendor renegotiation.
- **Promotional Strategy:** Promoted items generated higher immediate sales volume but showed variable margin efficiency depending on location category.
